Welcome to the endorsement desk

Today, you wear two hats.

An AI agent handles changes to motor insurance policies (in the trade, endorsements). It needs someone to talk to and someone to answer to. Both of those are you.

The customer 🧢

  1. Pick a demo case. It drafts your message and attaches the documents.

  3. Feel free to be difficult. Change your mind or contradict a document. It should cope.

Hat #2 · swap!

The approver 🎩

The agent can't send anything to the insurer without a human's sign-off. When it's ready, an approval card pops up in the chat and you become the reviewer.

  1. Approve and it goes to the insurer.
  2. Request changes or Reject with a reason, and the agent has to deal with it.
