Welcome to the endorsement desk

Today, you wear two hats.

An AI agent handles changes to motor insurance policies (in the trade, endorsements). It needs someone to talk to and someone to answer to. Both of those are you.

The paper trail

Trace and replay 🧾

Everything the agent did was written down as events. Open the Trace tab:

  1. Each row is a model call or a tool call, with when it started, how long it took and what it cost in tokens.
  2. Click a tool row to unfold its arguments and its result. Find record_slot to see a field being filled, or submit_to_insurer for the grand finale.
  3. Replay this session plays those same events back into the chat at fast-forward speed. Nothing is re-run and no model is called; it's the tape, not a remake.
